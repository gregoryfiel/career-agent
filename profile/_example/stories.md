# Interview stories — Ana Beatriz Moraes *(fictional)*

Format: **Result → Context → Action (1–2 decisions) → Result with number → Learning.**
Lead with the result. Always.

## Story A — The account nobody wanted
*Serves: difficult client · ownership · growth*

> **Result first:** "I took over the account the team considered unsalvageable and grew it from
> 4,200 to 11,000 followers in nine months."
>
> **Context:** "Six accounts, and one of them had been passed around three times. Engagement was
> flat and the client was threatening to leave."
>
> **Action 1:** "Before changing anything I read twelve months of their posts against the analytics
> and found the pattern — the two post formats that worked were the two we published least."
>
> **Action 2:** "I rebuilt the editorial calendar around those formats and cut the ones that never
> performed, which freed time rather than adding it."
>
> **Result:** "4,200 to 11,000 in nine months, and the client renewed."
>
> **Learning:** "I look at what the data already says before I propose anything new. The answer was
> in their own history."
